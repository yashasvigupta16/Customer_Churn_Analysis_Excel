# 📈Customer churn Analysis Dashboard (Excel)
An interactive Excel dashboard that analyses 7,043 telecom customers to uncover why customers leave, with business 
insights and retention recommendations.



## 🎯Problem Statement
Customer Churn directly hurts recurring revenue, and replacing a lost customer costs more than keeping one.
This project answers:
- Which customer segments churn the most?
- What factors(contract, tenure,services,payment method) drive churn?
- What should the business do to reduce it?

## 📊Key Metrics
| **Metric** | **Value** |
| ---- | ----- |
| Total Customers | 7,043 |
| Customers Lost | 1,869 |
| Overall Churn Rate | 26.54% |
| Avg Monthly Charges | $64.76 |
| Monthly Revenue | $4,56,117 |

## 🔍Key Findings
- **Contract Type:** Month-to-month customers churn at **42.71%** vs **11.27%**(one year) and **2.83%** (two year).
- **Senior Citizen:** Churn at **41.68%** vs **23.61%** for non-seniors( about 1.8x higher).
- **Internet Service:** Fiber optic users churn at **41.89%** vs **18.96%** for DSL, and **7.40%** for no internet.
- **Payment Method:** Electronic check users churn at **45.29%** far above other methods.
- **Tenure:** Churn peaks at **47.68%** at first year and falls to **6.61%** by year 6.
- **Monthly Charges:** Churn rises with price, from **8.97%** (0-20) to **36.91%** (80-100).

## 💼Business Implications
- Newer, month-to-month and high-paying customers are the most at risk, so early retention matters most.
- Senior churn may point to a support gap (possible reasons: limited phone support, harder account management). These are hypotheses,not proven by data.
- Electronic check and fiber optic segments need a closer look, as they may reflect payment friction or service issues.

## ✅Recommendations
1. Move month-to-month users to annual plans with discounts and loyalty offers.
2. Dedicated senior supports: phone helpline, simple language, callbacks.
3. Proactive outreach to new customers(first year) and before renewal.
4. Promote auto-pay over electronic check, with small incentives.
5. Investigate fiber optic service quality and pricing.
6. Run exits surveys to find out the real reasons customer leave.
